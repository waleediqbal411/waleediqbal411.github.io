01

I write software and papers about how the social world leaks into digital traces.

At Northeastern University London I lead work on measuring socio-economic inequality in large-scale online behaviour: who searches for what, which languages a platform serves well, how attention is distributed across geographies.

Previously, PhD at Queen Mary's Social Data Science Lab, where I was supervised by Dr. Ignacio Castro and Prof. Gareth Tyson in the Networks Research Group. Thesis on online manifestations of real-world inequalities.

Selected publications: Google Scholar · ORCID · ResearchGate.
