abstract: |
  ACM SIGCOMM has been a major research forum for fifty years and has had a major impact on the
  history of the Internet. On the 50th anniversary of SIGCOMM, we reflect upon its progress and
  achievements through the lens of its publication outlets — the SIGCOMM conference, IMC, CoNEXT,
  HotNets — and explore trends in co-authorship, country-based productivity, and knowledge flow
  to and from SIGCOMM venues using bibliometric techniques.
