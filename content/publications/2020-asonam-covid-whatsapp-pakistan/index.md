abstract: |
  The worldwide spread of COVID-19 prompted extensive online discussions, creating an infodemic
  on social media platforms such as WhatsApp and Twitter. We present the first analysis of
  COVID-19 discourse on public WhatsApp groups from Pakistan. Building on a large-scale annotation
  of thousands of messages containing text and images, we identify the main categories of
  discussion, focus on COVID-19 messages and inspect the different types of images/text being
  propagated, examine user behaviour to understand how misinformation spreads, and quantify the
  flow of information across WhatsApp and Twitter — showing how WhatsApp acts as a source for
  much of the information shared on Twitter.
