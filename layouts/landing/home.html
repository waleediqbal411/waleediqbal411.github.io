Waleed Iqbal

Assistant Professor in Data Science, Northeastern University London.

I study how socio-economic inequalities surface in online behaviour — from search queries and platform engagement to scholarly publishing. My PhD, supervised by Dr. Ignacio Castro and Prof. Gareth Tyson in the Social Data Science Lab, measured how real-world inequalities travel through the digital traces people leave behind.

Previously, I worked at IHSAN Lab in Information Technology University Lahore, Pakistan with Prof. Dr. Junaid Qadir as affiliated researcher and teaching assistant, and supervised by him as Postgraduate Student (MS Computer Science).
